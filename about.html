About Us - Smart Price Compare

Welcome to Smart Price Compare, a lightweight, independent online utility tool designed to help consumers make smarter, more budget-conscious decisions while grocery shopping across the globe, from India to the United States.

Our Mission: To empower everyday household shoppers with instant mathematical clarity, protecting their wallets against deceptive pricing tactics, hidden retail markups, and modern packaging tricks.

Why We Built This Tool

Modern retail corridors—whether online quick-commerce apps like Blinkit, Zepto, and BigBasket, or major global supermarkets like Walmart, Costco, Target, D-Mart, and Reliance Smart—frequently present prices in confusing configurations. Brands often use "shrinkflation" to cut down product weights while maintaining identical box sizes and price tags, making it incredibly hard to see what you are actually paying for.

We realized that calculating the cost per 100 grams, milliliters, ounces, or pounds mentally while standing in a busy grocery aisle is frustrating. This application was built as a clean, incredibly fast, and ad-supported open utility to perform those calculations for you instantly on any smartphone or mobile device.

Our Values

  • Transparency: Our tool relies entirely on strict mathematical logic. There are no hidden biases toward any specific retail brand, store, or product country.
  • Privacy First: We do not collect, track, or save any numbers, prices, or product information you type into our calculator. All computations happen locally inside your web browser.
  • Accessibility: We keep our code lightweight and fast so it loads instantly even on weak mobile networks or inside store aisles with low signal bars.
