About Us

Welcome to Smart Price Compare, a lightweight and independent online utility tool designed to help consumers make smarter, more budget-conscious decisions while grocery shopping in India.

Our Mission: To empower everyday household shoppers with instant mathematical clarity, protecting their wallets against deceptive pricing tactics and modern packaging tricks.

Why We Built This Tool

Modern retail corridors—whether online quick-commerce apps like Blinkit, Zepto, and BigBasket, or major offline supermarkets like D-Mart and Reliance Smart—frequently present prices in confusing formats. Brands often use "shrinkflation" to cut down product weights while maintaining identical box sizes and price tags.

We realized that calculating the cost per 100 grams or milliliters mentally while standing in a busy grocery aisle is inconvenient. This app was built as a clean, incredibly fast, and ad-supported open utility to perform those calculations for you instantly on any mobile device.

Our Values

  • Transparency: Our tool relies entirely on strict mathematical logic. There are no hidden biases toward any specific retail brand or product.
  • Privacy First: We do not collect, track, or save any numbers, prices, or product information you type into our calculator. All computations happen locally inside your web browser.
  • Accessibility: We keep our code lightweight and fast so it loads instantly even on weak 4G/3G mobile networks across India.
