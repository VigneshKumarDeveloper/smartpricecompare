Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

At Smart Price Compare, accessible from our website https://smartpricecompare.online, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document contains types of information that is collected and recorded by Smart Price Compare and how we use it.

1. Information We Collect

Our utility tool operates entirely on the client side (inside your web browser). We do not collect, store, or transmit any financial figures, weights, prices, or numbers you input into our comparison boxes to any external servers.

2. Log Files

Smart Price Compare follows a standard procedure of using log files. These files log visitors when they visit websites. The information collected by log files includes internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date and time stamp, referring/exit pages, and possibly the number of clicks. These are not linked to any information that is personally identifiable.

3. Cookies and Web Beacons

Like any other website, Smart Price Compare uses "cookies". These cookies are used to store information including visitors' preferences, and the pages on the website that the visitor accessed or visited. The information is used to optimize the users' experience by customizing our web page content based on visitors' browser type and/or other information.

4. Google DoubleClick DART Cookie & Third-Party Advertising

Google is one of the third-party vendors on our site. It also uses cookies, known as DART cookies, to serve ads to our site visitors based upon their visit to our site and other sites on the internet. Visitors may choose to decline the use of DART cookies by visiting the Google ad and content network Privacy Policy at the following URL: https://google.com.

Other third-party ad servers or ad networks use technologies like cookies, JavaScript, or Web Beacons that are used in their respective advertisements and links that appear on Smart Price Compare, which are sent directly to users' browsers. They automatically receive your IP address when this occurs. These technologies are used to measure the effectiveness of their advertising campaigns and/or to personalize the advertising content that you see on websites that you visit.

5. Third-Party Privacy Policies

Smart Price Compare's Privacy Policy does not apply to other advertisers or websites. Thus, we are advising you to consult the respective Privacy Policies of these third-party ad servers for more detailed information. It may include their practices and instructions about how to opt-out of certain options.

6. CCPA & GDPR Privacy Rights

We ensure full compliance with international and regional privacy laws. Users have the right to request access to, deletion of, or restriction on any tracking data collected via third-party cookies. You can choose to disable cookies through your individual browser options.

7. Consent

By using our website, you hereby consent to our Privacy Policy and agree to its Terms and Conditions.
