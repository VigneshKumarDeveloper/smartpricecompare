Smart Grocery Shopping in India:How to Use a Unit Price Calculator to Save Money

In India, navigating the grocery aisles can be confusing due to deceptive packaging. Whether you are shopping online via apps like Blinkit, Zepto, and BigBasket, or browsing the shelves at offline retail giants like D-Mart and Reliance Smart, brands often use clever marketing tactics to hide price hikes. This strategy, known as shrinkflation, involves reducing the product quantity while keeping the price and box size exactly the same.

Why Bigger Packs Aren't Always Cheaper

Most Indian shoppers assume that buying bulk or family-sized packs automatically guarantees a discount. However, a quick calculation often reveals the opposite. For instance, a 250g pack of tea leaves or paneer at your local Kirana store might actually cost less per gram than a 1kg mega pack from a major brand. Without calculating the exact unit cost, it is nearly impossible to spot these pricing anomalies.
