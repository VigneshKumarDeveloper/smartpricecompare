Smart Grocery Shopping Globally: How to Use a Unit Price Calculator to Save Money

Navigating the grocery aisles can be confusing due to deceptive packaging tactics. Whether you are shopping online via quick-commerce apps like Blinkit, Zepto, and BigBasket, or browsing the physical shelves at global retail giants like Walmart, Costco, Target, D-Mart, and Reliance Smart, brands often use clever marketing tricks to hide price hikes. This widespread strategy, known as shrinkflation, involves reducing the product quantity inside the box while keeping the price and packaging size exactly the same.

Why Bigger Packs Aren't Always Cheaper

Most everyday shoppers assume that buying bulk, club sizes, or family-sized packs automatically guarantees a wholesale discount. However, running a quick calculation frequently reveals the exact opposite. For instance, a standard 250g pack of tea leaves or paneer at your local Kirana store might actually cost less per gram than a 1kg mega pack from a major brand. Similarly, a small box of cereal at Target or Walmart can beat the unit rate of a massive wholesale warehouse bundle. Without calculating the exact unit cost, it is nearly impossible to spot these pricing anomalies.
